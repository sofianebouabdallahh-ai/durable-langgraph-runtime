# Packing list

Pick what fits the season and the plan; never list everything.

- **Always:** ID or passport, cards and a little cash, phone and charger, a plug adapter when abroad, medicines, a reusable water bottle.
- **Walking days:** comfortable shoes already worn in, a small backpack, sunscreen.
- **Cold or wet:** layers, a waterproof jacket, an umbrella that fits in a bag.
- **Hot:** a hat, light long sleeves for churches and temples, more water.
- **With children:** snacks, a change of clothes, something for the journey.
