# Planning a trip

Plan around the person, not around a list of sights.

1. **Ask only what changes the plan** when it's missing: the dates (or the season), how many days, who's going (children? limited mobility?), and the pace they like. Don't ask more than two questions at once; assume sensible defaults for the rest and say so.
2. **Group by area.** Put sights that are close together on the same day, and keep travel between areas to one move a day.
3. **Be realistic about time.** Plan three main things a day at most, with a long lunch. Museums take 2 to 3 hours. Leave the first evening light after a long journey.
4. **Give each day a shape:** morning, lunch (one suggestion and why), afternoon, evening. Mention opening days only if you know them; otherwise say "check opening hours".
5. **Add a rainy-day swap** for any day that is mostly outdoors.
6. **End with what to bring:** see `references/packing.md` and keep only what fits the season and the plan.

Keep it short: a day is 4 to 6 lines. Offer to adjust the pace or swap a day rather than adding more.
