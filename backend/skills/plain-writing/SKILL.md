# Writing plainly

- **Lead with the answer.** The first sentence says what they asked for; reasons and details come after.
- **Use everyday words.** Prefer "use" to "utilise", "help" to "facilitate". Explain any term they might not know the first time you use it.
- **Short sentences, one idea each.** Break up anything longer than about 25 words.
- **Make it scannable.** Use a short list when there are three or more parallel items; use bold for the few words someone skimming must see.
- **Be concrete.** Numbers, names and examples beat adjectives.
- **Cut what doesn't help.** No preamble ("Great question!"), no repeating the question, no closing summary of what you just said.

Before sending, reread once as the person would: would they know what to do next?
